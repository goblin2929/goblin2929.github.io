06 · How an Engagement Runs · Stages 1–2

How Do We Run a Client’s Engagement?

Novastacks runs each engagement as a four-stage cycle, the Compounding Loop: AEO/GEO/SEO Audit, Architect, Activate, Amplify. At each stage, the system performs the work at scale and the operator makes the judgement calls.

1AEO/GEO/SEO Audit

See where you stand and what needs attention.

We assess your search and AI visibility, competitor presence, content, technical foundations, backlinks and brand entity consistency. Your senior lead prioritises the gaps against your business goals.

You get: A visibility baseline, competitor comparison and prioritised findings across content, technical performance and brand authority.

2Architect

Decide the strategy and tactics.

We decide which buyer questions, products and markets to win first, and the tactics for each: content, technical fixes, link partnerships and entity management. Your senior lead sets the priorities and success measures.

You get: A strategy and tactics plan with agreed priorities, sequence and metrics.

06 · How an Engagement Runs · Stages 3–4

How Do We Run a Client’s Engagement?

3Activate

Put the plan into action.

We create and optimise content, implement technical fixes, develop link partnerships and strengthen brand information across relevant sources. Senior specialists guide outreach, verify claims and review execution.

You get: Completed site improvements, published content, partnership outreach and entity updates, with a tracker showing progress and pending actions.

4Amplify

See what changed and what comes next.

We track AI mentions, citations, search performance, acquired links and entity consistency. Your senior lead reviews progress against the baseline and decides what to expand, revise or stop.

You get: A performance report covering visibility, authority growth and next priorities, connected to traffic and lead enquiries.

Every cycle leaves you better equipped

Each engagement develops assets your business can keep using: stronger content, clearer customer insights and documented ways of working. We use performance findings to improve subsequent briefs and execution, so learning carries forward into the next round of work.
