04 · Agentic commerce

AI commerce will probably run three ways at once, and each one rewards something different.

Mode 01 · Walled garden

The platform's own agent, free to users

One company owns the model, browser and chatbot, and charges sellers to appear. It has little reason to show free, organic options.

10–50%fees built into every sale
Winning here takesBeing the answer the AI recommends unprompted, before any paid placement.
Mode 02 · Middleman layer

Shopify-style central catalogue

A shared product database that every agent can query. Buyers need to search less because the catalogue already holds the products.

5–25%per side
Winning here takesClean, structured product feeds that any agent can read and compare.
Mode 03 · Open agent to agent

The user's own agent talks to the seller's agent

Repeat purchases and local vendors, with no discovery step. Fulfilment, fraud and who pays for the agents are still open questions.

~0%platform fees
Winning here takesA seller agent and an API that a buyer's agent can trust and transact with.

05 · Be found, be chosen

There are three kinds of prompts, and most brands only show up in the one that matters least.

Discovery prompts

“I'm looking for a new perfume that's fresh and unique. What's good right now?”

The buyer has no brand in mind. This is the most important moment commercially, and it's where most brands don't appear.

High risk
Brands with no use-case content are missing here.
Consideration prompts

“Is Saide or Yuliya better for travel and mini perfumes?”

The buyer is building a shortlist. The AI's answer decides which brands stay on it.

Medium risk
Brands without structured comparison content lose here.
Intent prompts

“Where can I buy By Yuliya in Melbourne?”

The buyer is ready to purchase. Most brands do well here because the buyer has typed their name.

Low risk
Only if the brand made the shortlist in the first place.

05 · Be found, be chosen

Getting mentioned is not the same as getting recommended. These six reasons explain the gap.

01

Value

Named and praised, then given second place because of price.

04

Reputation

Sentiment on third-party review sites shifts the recommendation. An old crisis can linger in AI answers for years.

02

Baseline comparator

The AI uses the brand as the benchmark to compare others against, then recommends someone else.

05

Disintermediation

The AI tells the buyer to skip the retailer and buy direct from the brand.

03

Expert authority

The AI defers to a rival that clinicians or experts endorse.

06

Business model

Beaten on subscription lock-in, delivery options or flexibility.
