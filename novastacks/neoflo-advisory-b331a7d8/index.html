Growth & go-to-market · one-page advisory

Double down on what works, then hire to run it

A short read-out of our conversation, written so we can use our next conversation on decisions rather than background. It's based only on what we discussed, with none of Neoflo's data reviewed, so treat it as hypotheses to check against your pipeline.

You don't need a senior CMO. You need a system built on what already works, and an AI-forward operator to run it.

Your 8–9 well-known customers prove the product. Growth so far has come from referrals and roundtables, and the gaps are mostly execution: follow-up, messaging, and visibility. The best use of your budget is to strengthen the pillars that are working, hire a mid-level, AI-forward growth marketer to execute, and keep an advisor for the frameworks and test design.

01What we heard
Pipeline

Warm-only

Almost all deals come from referrals. Roundtables are the only channel that has reliably produced leads. There's no inbound or outbound motion yet.

Process

Person-driven

Deals depend on individual follow-up, not a system, so some fall through the cracks between SOW and pilot.

Buyers

Silent no's

Ghosting keeps happening. Finance buyers in particular rarely say no outright, so the real objections stay hidden.

Message

Strong product, weak landing

The product is good, but the pitch may not match how buyers want to hear it. You said yourself: "that might be the problem with us."

02Build on three pillars
Pillar 1 · Roundtables

Connect and amplify

Your one proven channel. Run it as a repeatable series rather than one-off events:

  • Before: build each invite list from your target customer profile
  • After: follow up with every attendee within 48 hours
  • Amplify: turn the discussion into posts, a short write-up and outreach to people who didn't attend
Pillar 2 · AI visibility

Be the answer buyers find

Finance and e-commerce leaders increasingly research vendors in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI answers. Referred buyers check you there too.

  • Find out where Neoflo shows up today for its key buyer questions
  • Publish proof (customer outcomes, roundtable insights) in formats AI tools cite
Pillar 3 · Pipeline intelligence

Learn from every conversation

Use AI to analyse sales calls and the full year of pipeline data against one framework:

  • Where deals stall, especially between SOW and pilot
  • The real objections behind ghosting, plus a 3-question lost-deal survey
  • The language buyers use, to rewrite the pitch

The guiding principle: having information in real time matters more than having it perfect. Weekly signal beats a quarterly report.

03Then test, using the numbers

Work backwards from the revenue target for each ICP tier: conversion rates, the SQLs you need, and acquisition cost. Then run each new channel as a 3-month test with a target set in advance, and keep, change or kill it based on the results.

04The right hire
Not this

A full-time senior CMO / director

Most of what's needed is execution, not strategy. Senior leaders often work at a different pace, cost a large share of the budget, and frequently aren't AI-native. The frameworks this role needs are now largely AI-assisted.

This

A mid-level, AI-forward B2B growth marketer

Senior-manager level, full-time, with an outbound and pipeline background. Numbers-driven, comfortable building structure, and fluent at giving AI tools business context and putting them to work. Source through referrals, with a 90-day scorecard written before hiring.

Brand consultant: limit her work to the foundations (ICP, positioning, messaging) with a deadline, so the new hire can put it into practice. Marketing analytics product idea: interview the two customers who asked about who would own it and who would pay, because CFOs and marketing teams are different buyers.
