Growth & go-to-market · one-page advisory

Double down on what works, then hire to run it

A short read-out of our conversation, written so we can use our next conversation on decisions rather than background. It's based only on what we discussed, without looking at Neoflo's data, services or product. The details will change once we do; the direction probably won't.

Put a focused budget behind the channels that already work, add one direct acquisition engine, and get senior marketing guidance for six months before making the big hire.

Your 8–9 well-known customers prove the product. With limited resources, budget $80–150K a year for marketing (10–15% of next year's ARR), commit $40K in the first quarter, build on roundtables and test AI visibility, outbound and social content, and work with an experienced marketing director for 6 months while you learn what the full-time role really needs.

01What we heard
Pipeline

Warm-only

Almost all deals come from referrals. Roundtables are the only channel that has reliably produced leads. There's no inbound or outbound motion yet.

Process

Person-driven

Deals depend on individual follow-up, not a system, so some fall through the cracks between SOW and pilot.

Buyers

Silent no's

Ghosting keeps happening. Finance buyers in particular rarely say no outright, so the real objections stay hidden.

Message

Strong product, weak landing

The product is good, but the pitch may not match how buyers want to hear it. You said yourself: "that might be the problem with us."

Map it first

Your proven channel

Map the true cost of each event and which deals it led to, then run them as a series: invite lists from your target customer profile, follow-up within 48 hours, and each discussion turned into content.

AI visibility (AEO)
$15Kper quarter

Amplify your positioning

Make sure the brand and messaging work you're paying for is what buyers find when they research the category in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI answers.

Outbound engine
$15Kper quarter

Direct acquisition test

The one channel where you can count leads directly. Use AI to learn from calls and pipeline data, then run sequences against the funnel math. Move it to the sales budget once it's working.

Social & content
$10Kper quarter

Keep the story visible

Turn roundtable insights, customer outcomes and your point of view into regular posts and content. That gives outbound something to point to, and gives AI tools something to cite.

04People: guidance now, the right hire next
Months 1–6

Pair with a marketing director

An experienced marketing director, working with you part-time, to map the roundtables, set up the AI-visibility work and outbound tests, and read the results. This uses those six months to learn which skills the full-time role really needs.

Then hire

A mid-level, AI-forward growth marketer

Not a full-time senior CMO, since most of the work is execution. Hire at senior-manager level: numbers-driven, outbound-savvy, and able to give AI tools business context and put them to work. Source through referrals.

Nipun

High-level decisions

Chooses which tests to run, sets the budget for each, and decides whether to keep, change or kill a channel. Keep the brand consultant focused on positioning, which the AI-visibility work then amplifies.
