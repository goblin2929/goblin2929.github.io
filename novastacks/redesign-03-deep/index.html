12Questions

Questions Singapore Brands Ask Us

Q1What is an AI SEO agency?+
An AI SEO agency optimizes your visibility in two places at once: traditional search engines like Google, and AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. A conventional SEO agency stops at Google rankings. Novastacks runs both as one practice, because the same content, structure, and authority signals increasingly feed both.
Q2What is AEO, and how is it different from SEO?+
SEO earns you rankings and clicks in Google's results. AEO earns you citations and recommendations inside AI-generated answers. SEO optimizes for the ten blue links; AEO optimizes for the moment ChatGPT or an AI Overview names a brand. You need both, and they reinforce each other.
Q3What is the difference between AEO and GEO?+
AEO and GEO both make your content visible to AI-driven search but differ in focus. AEO targets direct, concise answers such as voice search and featured snippets. GEO optimizes for comprehensive, authoritative content that AI models summarize and cite. Both matter, and they work best together.
Q4How do I choose an SEO agency in Singapore?+
Look for three things: senior operators who actually run your account rather than juniors, transparency about who does the work, and a methodology that covers AI search and not just Google. Ask any agency how they will get you cited by ChatGPT and Perplexity. If they have no clear answer, they are optimizing for a shrinking slice of search.
Q5How do you get a brand cited by ChatGPT and AI search?+
AI answer engines pull from structured, authoritative, clearly formatted content. We add FAQ and Q&A structured data, build comparison and use-case pages that answer buyer questions, strengthen author and brand credentials, and surface citeable data. We then track which prompts cite you and refine. In one recent engagement this took a logistics SaaS from zero to 43 AI citations in 60 days.
Q6What makes Novastacks different from other SEO agencies in Singapore?+
Two things. First, we run SEO, AEO, and GEO as one system, so your Google rankings and AI citations compound together. Second, senior operators run your account, with experience at Tencent, Expedia, and Klook and a Google Product Expert leading AEO, backed by an AI agent team that scales execution.
Q7How much does SEO cost in Singapore?+
SEO engagements in Singapore vary widely by scope and how competitive your category is. Rather than a fixed package, we scope each engagement to your goals after a free audit. Book a strategy call and we will give you a clear, specific proposal.
Q8Do you work with businesses outside Singapore?+
Yes. We are Singapore based and know this market deeply, but we also work with APAC companies and businesses in other markets and segments such as SaaS, financial, OTA, and e-commerce. AEO and GEO are global by nature: if your buyers ask AI about your category anywhere, we can help you become the cited answer.
