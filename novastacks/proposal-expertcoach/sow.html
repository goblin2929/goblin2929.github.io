1 Background and objective

ExpertCoach publishes approximately 16 content pages and is losing share of voice in AI-assistant answers to CredentialPrep and CoachCertify, both executing on greater volume and cleaner technical structure. The Client operates as a single founder and is constrained by production capacity, not by strategy.

The objective is to increase the frequency with which ExpertCoach is mentioned and cited by ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity in response to the questions its buyers actually ask — measured against a fixed prompt set and a baseline set in week two.

2 Scope of services

2.1 Discovery and baseline — weeks 1–2

  • Technical and AEO audit — crawlability, indexation, architecture, internal linking, structured data and machine-readability of existing pages.
  • Prompt set construction using our prompt methodology, simulating how ICF-track coaches ask assistants about certification and provider choice.
  • Keyword and content-gap database built from the Client's Search Console data and first-party inputs, not generic keyword lists.
  • Competitor citation teardown — which CredentialPrep and CoachCertify pages are cited, for which prompts, and why structurally.
  • Baseline report and dashboard configuration across all four assistants, handed over with Client login. Platform licence billed separately — clause 6.

2.2 Technical and structural optimisation

  • Prioritised remediation specification — every audit issue ranked by impact, written implementation-ready for the Client's Astro codebase.
  • Architecture design — hub-and-spoke structure, URL taxonomy and internal linking built for machine retrieval, not human browsing.
  • Structured data specification across content and service pages.
  • Ongoing technical monitoring throughout the term. We identify and specify faults proactively; the Client need not detect or report them.

2.3 Content production — 60 pages over four months

  • 60 pages — new content plus structural refreshes of the existing 16 posts, averaging 15 pages per month.
  • Brief, draft, review, publish — researched against the gap database, drafted to a citation-oriented structure, supplied in Google Docs for approval, then published.
  • Monthly refresh cycle on published pages, driven by which pages earn citations and which competitor pages beat our drafts in AI answers.

2.4 Measurement and reporting

  • Daily AI-visibility tracking on the fixed prompt set across all four assistants, answers retained so movement can be explained, not just observed. We configure, operate and interpret the platform; the Client licenses it.
  • Client dashboard, live throughout: share of voice, citations, competitor position, and the verbatim answers assistants give about ExpertCoach.
  • Monthly report and 60-minute strategy session — what was delivered, what moved, the reasoning, and next month's plan.
  • Off-page and community signals — where third-party citations, forums or review platforms influence AI answers here, we surface them and recommend action for the Client to execute.

3 Exclusions

Expressly outside this SOW; may be quoted separately.

  • Off-page execution — link acquisition, digital PR, guest placement, review-platform seeding. Recommendations are given under 2.4; execution is not.
  • Community outreach execution — Reddit, forum and community participation.
  • Software development — we specify technical fixes; the Client implements them in its Astro repository.
  • Paid media — search or social advertising management, and media spend.
  • Non-English content, translation and localisation.
  • Third-party tool licences — the AI-visibility tracking platform, licensed by the Client directly. Priced at clause 6.
