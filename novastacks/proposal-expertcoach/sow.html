1 Background and objective

ExpertCoach publishes approximately 16 content pages and is losing share of voice in AI-assistant answers to CredentialPrep and CoachCertify, both executing on greater volume and cleaner technical structure. The Client operates as a single founder and is constrained by production capacity, not strategy — so this engagement is scoped as done-for-you.

The objective is to increase the frequency with which ExpertCoach is mentioned and cited by ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity in response to the questions its buyers ask — measured against a fixed prompt set and a week-two baseline.

2 Scope of services

2.1 Discovery and baseline — weeks 1–2

  • Technical and AEO audit — crawlability, indexation, architecture, internal linking, structured data and machine-readability.
  • Prompt set construction using our prompt methodology, simulating how ICF-track coaches ask assistants about certification and provider choice.
  • Keyword and content-gap database built from the Client's Search Console data and first-party inputs.
  • Competitor citation teardown — which CredentialPrep and CoachCertify pages are cited, for which prompts, and why.
  • Baseline report and dashboard configuration across all four assistants, handed over with Client login. Platform licence is a suggested add-on — clause 6.

2.2 Technical implementation — done for you

  • We implement, you approve. With publishing access to the Astro site we ship the fixes ourselves and notify the Client of every change. Client development time is never the bottleneck.
  • Implementation documentation — every change ranked by impact and written up step by step, so the Client holds a record of what was altered and why.
  • Architecture and structured data — hub-and-spoke structure, URL taxonomy, internal linking and schema, built for machine retrieval rather than human browsing.
  • Ongoing technical monitoring throughout the term. We identify, fix and report; the Client need not detect faults.

2.3 Content production — minimum 60 pages, no cap

  • Minimum 60 pages across the term — a floor, not a ceiling. Where a typical agency quotes four or five pages a month and stops at the retainer line, we keep producing and refreshing while the citation data shows ground to take.
  • Brief, draft, review, publish — researched against the gap database, drafted to a citation-oriented structure, supplied in Google Docs for approval, then published directly by us.
  • Refreshed as many times as needed. Pages are revisited whenever the data shows a competitor page cited over ours. Refreshes are not counted against the 60.

2.4 Measurement and reporting

  • Daily AI-visibility tracking on the fixed prompt set across all four assistants, answers retained so movement can be explained, not just observed. We configure, operate and interpret the platform; the Client licenses it.
  • Client dashboard, live throughout: share of voice, citations, competitor position, and the verbatim answers assistants give about ExpertCoach.
  • Monthly written report and 60-minute strategy call — what was delivered, what moved, the reasoning, and next month's plan.
  • A shared working channel alongside the report and call, so changes are visible as they happen, not a month later.
  • Off-page and community signals — where third-party citations, forums or review platforms influence AI answers, we surface them and recommend action.

3 Not included

  • Off-page and backlink acquisition — link building, digital PR, guest placement, review-platform seeding. Available as an add-on, clause 6.
  • Community outreach execution — Reddit, forum and community participation.
  • Paid media — search or social advertising management, and media spend.
  • Non-English content, translation and localisation.
