02The bet

Surface area is your bottleneck

ExpertCoach has around 16 published posts and some indexation problems. Your competitors aren't beating you on quality — they're beating you on volume, structure and machine-readability. AI assistants cite what they can crawl, parse and trust, and right now there simply isn't enough of you to cite.

Over four months we take you from fewer than 30 content pages to roughly 90, all built to be retrieved and quoted by an assistant rather than skimmed by a human.

That's ~15 pages a month — the same cadence I run for clients paying three to five times this. It's affordable here because your niche is narrow: the research compounds fast, and every page we build makes the next one cheaper.

05The test

How you'll judge whether this worked

We lock a baseline in week two across a fixed set of tracked prompts. The target for month four is a 50% increase in AI visibility — how often ExpertCoach is mentioned or cited across that prompt set. Same prompts, same platforms, measured daily, visible to you throughout. Nothing gets moved after the fact.

For calibration: a US-market client of ours started at 0.3% AI visibility against strong competitors. We set a three-month target of 3% — and hit it within the first month of publishing.

I'm not promising you that number. Your market is narrower and your competitors are sharper. But 50% over four months is a floor I'm comfortable being held to.

If we miss it

You still finish with a technically clean site, 60 pages you own, a keyword and prompt database, and four months of daily tracking data. You'd be making the next decision with evidence instead of a hunch — and you can walk away with all of it.
