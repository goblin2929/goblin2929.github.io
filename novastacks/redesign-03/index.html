Questions Singapore Brands Ask Us

What is an AI SEO agency?
An AI SEO agency optimizes your visibility in two places at once: traditional search engines like Google, and AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. A conventional SEO agency stops at Google rankings. Novastacks runs both as one practice, because the same content, structure, and authority signals increasingly feed both.
What is AEO, and how is it different from SEO?
SEO (Search Engine Optimization) earns you rankings and clicks in Google's results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) earns you citations and recommendations inside AI-generated answers. SEO optimizes for the ten blue links; AEO optimizes for the moment ChatGPT or an AI Overview names a brand. You need both, and they reinforce each other.
What is the difference between AEO and GEO?
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) both make your content visible to AI-driven search, but they differ in focus. AEO targets direct, concise answers, such as voice search and featured snippets. GEO optimizes for comprehensive, authoritative content that AI models summarize and cite. Both matter, and they work best together.
How do I choose an SEO agency in Singapore?
Look for three things: senior operators who actually run your account rather than juniors, transparency about who does the work, and a methodology that covers AI search and not just Google. Ask any agency how they will get you cited by ChatGPT and Perplexity. If they have no clear answer, they are optimizing for a shrinking slice of search.
When should my business engage an SEO agency?
Engage one when organic search is, or should be, a meaningful channel for you and you do not have senior in-house SEO capacity. For AEO specifically, earlier is better: AI answer engines are still forming their view of most brands, so there is a first-mover window to become the cited source in your category before competitors do.
How do you get a brand cited by ChatGPT and AI search?
AI answer engines pull from structured, authoritative, clearly formatted content. We add FAQ and Q&A structured data, build comparison and use-case pages that directly answer buyer questions, strengthen author and brand credentials, and surface citeable data. We then track which prompts cite you and refine from there. In one recent engagement this took a logistics SaaS from zero to 43 AI citations in 60 days.
