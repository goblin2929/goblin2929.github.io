One scan, four answers

What does a DEXA scan measure?

DEXA (dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry) passes two low-dose X-ray beams through the body and measures how each tissue absorbs them. Unlike a weighing scale or bioimpedance device, it produces a region-by-region map of your body — not a single estimate.

Body fat percentage

Total and regional fat mass, including the trunk-versus-limb distribution that matters for metabolic risk.

Visceral fat

The fat around your organs — the strongest body-composition signal for cardiometabolic disease.

Lean muscle mass

Region-by-region muscle measurement, the number to defend as you age.

Bone mineral density

Screens for osteopenia and osteoporosis years before a fracture would reveal them.

Your T-score, in plain words

How your bone density compares with a healthy 30-year-old's. Above −1.0 is healthy; below −2.5 is osteoporosis. If you sit in between, we set a re-scan interval and a prevention plan rather than waiting for a fracture to tell you first.

Visceral fat is the number to watch

The fat around your organs predicts metabolic risk better than weight or BMI. If it's elevated, the doctor walks you through the specific levers — training, nutrition, sleep — and how fast this number realistically moves.

Muscle is your retirement account

Lean mass falls with age, and DEXA shows exactly where. We benchmark you against your age band and set the number to defend at your next scan.

Choosing the right tool

DEXA vs InBody: which is accurate?

Bioimpedance scales (InBody and similar) estimate composition from an electrical current, so results move with hydration, food and time of day. DEXA measures tissue directly.

What to expect

How your DEXA appointment works

Wear comfortable clothing without metal. No fasting needed; skip calcium supplements for 24 hours.

Lie still on an open scanning table for 10–15 minutes. No enclosure, no injections, very low radiation.

A doctor reviews your scan the same visit — fat, muscle, visceral fat and bone density, region by region.

Leave with a written baseline and, if you choose, a re-test schedule tied to your longevity plan.

Straight answers

DEXA scans, frequently asked

How much does a DEXA scan cost in Singapore?

Pricing varies by clinic and by whether the scan is standalone or part of a screening programme. We confirm current pricing when you book — WhatsApp +65 8019 0641 for the rate and available times.

Is a DEXA scan safe?

DEXA uses a very low dose of X-ray — a fraction of a standard chest X-ray. It is considered safe for routine, repeated use in adults, though it is not recommended during pregnancy.

How often should I repeat it?

Every 3–6 months for body-composition tracking while actively changing training or nutrition; every 1–2 years for bone-density monitoring, depending on your baseline and risk factors.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is needed. Book directly with the clinic and our doctors will take your history at the appointment.
