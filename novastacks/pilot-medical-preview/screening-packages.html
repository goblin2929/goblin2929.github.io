Straight answers

Screening packages, frequently asked

How much does health screening cost at Pilot Medical Clinic?

Age-targeted programmes run from SGD 659 (Essential, 30–40) to SGD 1,496 (Comprehensive, 61+). Organ-targeted screens start at SGD 400, pre-marital checks at SGD 239, and the Advance tiers range from SGD 290 to SGD 1,800. Every price on this page is the full programme price.

Which package should I choose for my age?

Our age-targeted programmes follow the risks of each decade: Essential (30–40) sets your metabolic baseline, Vitality (41–50) adds early-detection markers and imaging, Longevity (51–60) adds structural health such as bone density, and Comprehensive (61+) is a full systemic review. Unsure? The pre-screening consultation settles it before anything is billed.

What happens on screening day?

Programmes include a pre-screening consultation, the tests for your package, and a post-screening review where a doctor walks you through every result. Most programmes complete in a single morning at Camden Medical Centre.

Can I add tests to a package?

Yes. Common add-ons include ultrasounds (SGD 200), bilateral breast ultrasound or mammogram from SGD 225, bone mineral density (SGD 166), vitamin D (SGD 125), STD panels (from SGD 72), genetic tests from SGD 380, and ctDNA multi-cancer testing. Your doctor will advise which add-ons your history actually justifies.
