What is longevity medicine?

Most healthcare waits for symptoms. Longevity medicine measures how your body is actually ageing — through genetic, epigenetic, metabolic and fitness markers — and acts on those signals before they become disease. Straight answers

Longevity medicine, frequently asked

What is longevity medicine?

Longevity medicine is preventive care that measures how your body is actually ageing — through genetic, epigenetic, metabolic and fitness markers — and uses those measurements to guide treatment before disease develops, rather than after symptoms appear.

How is a longevity assessment different from a normal health screening?

A standard screening checks whether disease is present today. A longevity assessment adds markers of how fast you are ageing — biological age, body composition, cardiorespiratory fitness and inflammatory markers — and turns them into a personalised plan you re-test against over time.

Who is the PILOT ONE programme for?

Adults who want a preventive, data-driven picture of their health — typically from their mid-30s onward, and anyone with a family history of heart disease, cancer or metabolic conditions who wants earlier warning than routine checkups provide.
