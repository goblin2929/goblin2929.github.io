TMS Solutions Compared: What Freight Forwarders Should Look For

Choosing a transport management system is a multi-year commitment. The platforms in this comparison differ most in three areas: depth of customs integration, how rating and quoting are automated, and whether the system runs your whole operation or bolts onto an existing stack.

Pricing that scales with your operation

Three transparent tiers — Launch, Scale, and Enterprise — priced per active user. Every tier includes AI-assisted workflow automation for rating, quoting, and customs filing.

Where an integrated platform wins

  • One data model across forwarding, warehousing, and accounting — no re-keying between systems.
  • Native customs filing in 60+ jurisdictions, updated as regulations change.
  • Productivity analytics that show where branch teams lose time.

Point solutions can be a reasonable start for a small brokerage, but most forwarders outgrow them once volumes pass a few hundred shipments a month…
