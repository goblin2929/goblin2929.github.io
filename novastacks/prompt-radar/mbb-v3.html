Prompt Radar Pro (PRP)

Prompt Radar Pro (PRP) is Novastacks' proprietary system for identifying the prompts most likely to influence AI-driven discovery, consideration, and purchase — and proving whether the brand is cited on them.

It's built on three proprietary intelligence layers — first-party buyer signal, search demand, and product-market fit — and two deterministic engines: a generation engine that grounds demand into a topical knowledge graph and fans out the real questions AI engines ask, and a prioritization engine that ranks them to the tracked set.

Every prompt is observed from how engines actually retrieve — never guessed.
