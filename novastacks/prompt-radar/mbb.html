Prompt Radar Pro (PRP)

Prompt Radar Pro (PRP) is Novastacks' proprietary system for identifying the prompts most likely to influence AI-driven discovery, consideration, and purchase — and proving whether the brand is cited on them.

It's built on three proprietary intelligence layers — first-party buyer signal, search demand, and product-market fit — and two deterministic engines: a generation engine that grounds demand into a topical knowledge graph and fans out the real questions AI engines ask, and a prioritization engine that ranks them to the tracked set.

Every prompt is observed from how engines actually retrieve — never guessed.

// engine_2  Prioritization
gate → rank → diversify

Applies product-market fit to score the universe down to the tracked tier, by a fixed, coded rule. It removes opinion: the same inputs always produce the same priorities, and every tracked prompt is non-branded, likely to earn a recommendation, and spread across the funnel.

1 · gate
Keep only non-branded questions where engines actually name vendors. The rest route to the content plan.
2 · rank
Order by demand × winnability × product & buyer fit. A high-intent, low-volume question can still reach the top.
3 · diversify
Spread across the funnel; collapse any two prompts that are really one bet (same answer, same sources).
