PRELIMINARY ANALYSIS · 4 JULY 202615 / 41
Singtel · Executive Summary

Singtel leads Singapore telco organic search — but that SEO lead hasn't become an AI-era advantage yet.

AI already names Singtel in 67% of buyer questions. Yet 71% of the sources AI cites about the brand are third-party, and Singtel's content is thinner than StarHub's. Four fixable gaps stand between today's search lead and an AI-era lead.

01 · Content Leadership

StarHub owns the how-tos buyers search.

StarHub earns 2.8× Singtel's article traffic from fewer pages by covering telco how-tos — scam-number lookup, WiFi fixes, IDD. Singtel's blog runs on appliance reviews.

Fix → win the telco how-to track.

02 · Technical Foundation

Singtel's best product pages can't be read by AI.

The phone pages that lead the market on traffic hide price, plans and specs from crawlers — the content loads only in the browser.

Fix → server-side render + valid schema.

03 · AEO Citation

Known everywhere, cited nowhere at the top of the funnel.

AI names Singtel 67% of the time but cites third parties 71% of the time. The leak is worst at awareness, where new customers discover the category — Singtel owns just 5% of citations there.

Fix → convert brand awareness into citations.

04 · Entity Reputation

The "Singtel" conversation is owned by complaints, not by Singtel.

Branded search and Reddit are dominated by outages — "is singtel down" is 3,500/mo — with no official page answering them, so third parties fill the gap.

Fix → publish the answers, give AI a Singtel source.
