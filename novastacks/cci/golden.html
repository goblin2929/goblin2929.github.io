Golden clusters (P0)

The clusters to focus on for in-depth analysis. High relevance + real search demand + we're absent = a P0 candidate — the ranking proposes, you confirm (a confirmed P0 is what deep capture and AMB briefs key on). Rival supply is shown as context — it never gates or sorts this list (thin rival supply can be the best news). Demand figures are est., dataforseo; our-rank signals are labeled by source; coverage joins arrive with the Content Cluster Registry Phase-1 join.

Two demand columns, two different kinds of number. Volume/mo and KD are DataForSEO's estimate of the market — they exist only for keywords some tracked domain already ranks for. Our GSC demand is measured: impressions, clicks and best position our own site recorded in Search Console, joined to the cluster by its CC- ref. They answer different questions, so they are never added and never substituted for each other. Rank by our measured demand · default ranking

Cluster names on this page come from the Content Cluster Registry (270 clusters, 22 golden) — quote the CC- ref. Rename a cluster in the registry and every view here follows; nothing else needs re-running. A row marked unregistered is still showing its own stored name, which can drift.
