01 · Bio

Bio.

Founder & CEO
Novastacks

Singapore

Tina Chu is a CMO turned AI builder. She is the founder and CEO of Novastacks, a Singapore-based AI-driven marketing agency specialising in AI search, which also helps businesses with marketing AI transformation.

She knows the pressure her audience is under. For 20 years she carried the CEO's demand for results while budgets were capped, and led marketing teams through it with strategy rather than headcount, most recently a 100-person growth organisation at Traveloka as VP of Acquisition Marketing. More than 200 marketers worked for her across Expedia, Tencent, Klook and Traveloka, and her work for Hotels.com won the Marketing Excellence Awards twice.

At Novastacks she designs how a marketing organisation should run with AI: what the agents do, where human judgment stays, and how the output ties to pipeline and revenue. She builds the systems herself so the design is tested in a live business. She works directly with client C-suites on growth strategy, and teaches marketers and leaders how to pivot their skills to AI through masterclasses with SMU Academy, corporates and business organisations.

She holds an MBA from the Wharton School.
