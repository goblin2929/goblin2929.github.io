04The 2026 cold-outreach playbook, applied

1 · Deliverability before copy. Dedicated sending subdomain, SPF/DKIM/DMARC aligned, 2-week warm-up. Gmail/Yahoo bulk rules: one-click unsubscribe, spam <0.3%. Verify the list before send; suppress role accounts; throttle 200–400/day.
2 · Plain-text format. No HTML template, no images, one main CTA. The 18.7%-open March send was HTML; plain-text from a person should clear 30%+ on an affinity list.
3 · Sender: Trenton Chen, CEO. CEO-signed reads owner-to-owner and matches the Gong finding that the owner is the economic buyer. Replies triaged daily from a monitored inbox. Never marketing@.
4 · Personalization. {{first name}}, {{company}}, "WCA" in subject or first line; cargo-type variant line (ocean vs air splits) per the 2 Jul meeting directive.
5 · One main CTA per email. Email 1 brand intro → LP · Email 2 pain + pricing → 15-min call (supporting link: CargoWise-pricing blog) · Email 3 AI → demo ask. Standalone broadcasts: each email is fully self-contained.
6 · Compliance. CAN-SPAM footer. WCA is global: wave 1 = US + Canada members (ICP fit AND compliance-safe); EU only after legitimate-interest sign-off.
7 · System tie-in. Clickers/repliers imported to HubSpot → the rebuilt Organic nurture takes over. UTM wca_broadcast matches 2021 tracking so results are comparable.
